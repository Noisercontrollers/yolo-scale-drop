Declaration of Interest / Competing Interest
============================================

(For the Editorial Manager submission form field "Declaration of Interest",
and/or as a section in the manuscript before the References.)

The authors declare that they have no known competing financial interests or personal relationships that could have appeared to influence the work reported in this paper.

Notes:
- If any author has a competing interest (employment, consultancy, patents,
  funding, honoraria, etc. within the last 3 years that could affect the work),
  replace the sentence above with a concrete disclosure.
- Add funding acknowledgements in the separate "Acknowledgements" / "Funding"
  field or section: e.g. "This research did not receive any specific grant from
  funding agencies in the public, commercial, or not-for-profit sectors."
